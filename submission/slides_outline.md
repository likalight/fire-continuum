# Seven-slide presentation outline

1. **Did burning increase—or did the satellite change?** Show the same year, same place, Aqua versus NOAA-20. Introduce Fire Continuum.
2. **A history worth preserving.** Explain the user: a regional analyst comparing seasons across decades. Show NASA's November 2026 Suomi NPP transition notice. Avoid claiming user interviews or operational adoption.
3. **45 NASA files, one inspectable calendar.** Indonesia, 2003–2025. Show the calendar, source labels, area selector and original detections.
4. **Bridge the sensors transparently.** Aqua reference; common 1 km cell-days; regional/quarter calibration; one primary satellite per period. Explain what a hotspot is and is not.
5. **Test on years the fit never saw.** NOAA-20 2022–2025: 451.0% unadjusted WAPE, 15.5% single-factor baseline, 13.5% regional-seasonal adjustment. NPP replication: 13.7%. State: agreement with Aqua, not all-fire accuracy.
6. **Read a season, export the evidence.** Live path: September 2023 → choose Sumatra → inspect seasonal profile → compare sensors → download analytics and original points. Explain the historical median and coverage limitations.
7. **Keep the history useful as satellites change.** Summarize delivered prototype and honest gaps: 2026 full archive, coverage normalization, independent validation and global expansion. Provide public demo, source and NASA attribution.

This is an outline, not a rendered presentation. Keep the eventual deck to seven slides and check the official submission form's current instructions.
