# Seven-slide alternative — content outline

The finished submission demo is the 30-second MP4; this is an alternative outline, not a rendered deck.

1. **Read the fire season.** The practical question: is a changing hotspot count a changing burning pattern, or a changing satellite?
2. **Reveal a real fire.** Southern Borneo, 15 September 2019. Show natural color, SWIR, then 16 later NOAA-20 centres. State the 3 h 47 m offset; curated event corroboration, not count accuracy.
3. **Connect the record.** One platform per period, common cell-days, regional/seasonal overlap calibration. Preserve original observations. The calendar spans Indonesia, 2003–2025.
4. **Try to break the conclusion.** Kalimantan September 2019: adjusted 1.93× versus Aqua 2.15× historical median. All six variants retain above typical; the extreme category is sensitive. Show held-out errors and failed transfer tests.
5. **Bring November into context.** November 1–13, 2026 compared with identical dates. Kalimantan adjusted 6.03× versus Aqua 6.76×; 10.8% discrepancy. All 26 source files available, observation completeness unknown. This does not solve the earlier five-day 46.8% gap.
6. **Make it usable and auditable.** Four views, plain-language glossary, observation-aware calendar, exact-date original-point CSV, downloadable analysis ZIP with hashes and selected state. Show one investigation question, not an emergency instruction.
7. **What comes next.** Practitioner evaluation, paired clear-sky coverage, broader geographic validation and continuous 2026 ingestion. Link the live app, handbook, code and sources. NASA does not endorse this prototype.
