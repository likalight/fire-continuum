# Seven-slide alternative outline

This is a content outline, not a finished presentation. The submission demo is the 30-second MP4.

1. **The satellite changed. Should your conclusion?** Same Kalimantan month: Aqua 13,503 versus unadjusted NPP 54,100 occupied cell-days. Different instruments can change the apparent story.
2. **A usable historical record.** A 23-year Indonesia calendar with area and date selection, original measurements, and exported analytics and sources.
3. **Harmonize, then test the decision.** Fit earlier years and freeze methods. NOAA-20 agreement rises from 79.6% to 82.9% across 240 region-months, but rare-category misses rise from 1 to 3 of 6. Aqua agreement is not ground-truth accuracy.
4. **Does it travel?** Punjab category agreement rises from 60.4% to 70.8% after local adaptation. In southeast Australia, count error improves while category agreement falls from 72.9% to 66.7%. Show both outcomes; do not claim global validation.
5. **What could the satellite see?** 167 daily FireMask/QA layers and 17 missing dates; 12 Landsat subsets across 6 sample cells, with all fire-attribution outcomes inconclusive. Show real dates and haze.
6. **From evidence to investigation.** A four-question case brief, 3 transparent review candidates, inspectable imagery and downloadable evidence. Retrospective analysis, not response prioritization or prediction.
7. **What is ready, what comes next.** Public app, reproducible scripts, source hashes, automated tests and a 30-second demo. The recent 46.8% gap remains unresolved; practitioner validation is deferred. Paired clear-sky coverage and broader transfer remain research tasks.
