# Fire Continuum

NASA Space Apps 2026: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots.

- Primary audience: regional fire analysts and land managers; initial geography: Indonesia.
- NASA FIRMS annual MODIS / VIIRS S-NPP / NOAA-20 records are observations, not distinct fires or burned area.
- Preserve raw sensor identity, collection/version, date, confidence and source URLs.
- Never add overlapping satellites together to imply a trend. Use one documented source per period.
- Harmonization must be evaluated on years excluded from fitting. Report residual error and scope honestly.
- Missing archives/coverage are not zero fire. No live evacuation or safety recommendations.
- Raw files are ignored. Publish small derived data, exact methods, validation and reproducible scripts.
- GitHub repositories must be under likalight. Preserve SinkingCities separately.
