Source and scope

The official challenge Details and Resources tabs were read live on 14 November 2026 and checked against the user-supplied full statement. Resources links to NASA FIRMS. The challenge is not restricted to Indonesia and does not require AI. The central task remains harmonization plus an understandable regional, temporal burning-activity calendar.

Added geographic and AI capabilities

The geographic workbench loads three national histories compiled from 90 annual files, worldwide recent observations, arbitrary-date daily analytics, original point downloads, reusable package import/export and shareable analysis links. A local sentence-embedding assistant retrieves explanations tied to the current selection, with source citations. It is not generative chat. Its development intent fixtures pass 22/25 after tuning, with two withheld answers and one wrong-topic route; no external user accuracy is claimed.

Full geographic method, validation, schema and AI limitations.

What remains partial

Continuous 2026 history, worldwide harmonized historical coverage, paired clear-sky/overpass normalization, full sensor interchangeability and external practitioner validation remain incomplete. The world map represents recent observations only. Imported packages are not authenticated scientific products. Arbitrary-day analytics are implemented for available supplied-country dates, but arbitrary-day historical anomaly estimation is not inferred automatically. The 46.8% earlier short-window discrepancy remains unresolved.
