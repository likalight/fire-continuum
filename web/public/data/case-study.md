# Kalimantan, September 2019: a corroborated event and a qualified seasonal conclusion

**What we verified:** NASA published a separate-instrument Landsat 8 OLI observation of active fires in southern Borneo during September 2019. This independently corroborates event existence at the regional/monthly scale. It does not independently validate our aggregate counts, fire boundaries, attribution or anomaly magnitude.

Source: [NASA Earth Observatory, Smoke Blankets Borneo, 18 September 2019](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/smoke-blankets-borneo-145614/). NASA's Landsat image is dated 15 September 2019. The image uses shortwave-infrared bands 7-6-2 over natural-color 4-3-2 to show active fires. Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Joshua Stevens; Landsat data from USGS. Article by Adam Voiland. The original annotated image is displayed unchanged; it is not an AI-generated illustration or an independently georeferenced overlay.

Image URL, SHA-256, observation date, retrieval date and interpretation limits: `case-evidence.json`. The 2025 date in the NASA website's image upload path is not the acquisition date. The article and day-of-year 258 in the filename identify the 2019 observation.

## What the calendar establishes

Study rectangle: 108–120 E, -5–4.5 N, Indonesia archive only. Primary source: NPP. Calibration years: 2013–2017. The 2019 case is outside those fitting years.

Historical September Aqua baseline: 2003–2017, n=15, median 6,269, 90th percentile 14,577.8. Comparing unadjusted NPP with that Aqua median produces an invalid cross-sensor ratio of 8.63×. The app shows that comparison as a demonstration of the measurement problem, not a valid anomaly.

All six predeclared pipeline variants retain above-median 2019 activity in both sensors. Five stay below their corresponding 90th percentile; the high-confidence-only variant crosses it. “Fires occurred” and “above typical” are supported; “unusually high” is filter-dependent. A serious fire event does not have to be a historically unprecedented month.

Thirteen seven-day UTC windows (1 August–30 October) show the 2019 and 2020 seasonal progression beside a 2003–2017 Aqua weekly median. October 31 is omitted to avoid an incomplete final week. Whole-country cell-day deduplication precedes region selection, consistent with the monthly processing convention. Missing clear-sky coverage remains unknown. The 2020 comparison is not assumed fire-free.

## What would strengthen this further

Georeferenced before/after reflectance, systematically sampled burn/non-burn controls, field or independently mapped fire perimeters, and cloud/overpass denominators would test additional claims. They are not presented as completed work. MODIS-derived burned-area products are not fully independent of active-fire inputs.
